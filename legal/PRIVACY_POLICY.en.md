# 🛡️ Privacy Policy – FinFlow

**Last Updated**: July 2025  
**Version**: 1.0

FinFlow is committed to protecting user privacy in accordance with GDPR, Israeli Privacy Protection Law (1981), and Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards (PCI-DSS).

---

## 📊 What Information Do We Collect?

### Personal Information Actively Collected:
- **Basic Data**: Full name, email address, phone number
- **Financial Information**: Bank account details, credit cards (encrypted)
- **Business Data**: Company name, business type, tax ID
- **User Preferences**: Language settings, display customizations

### Technical Information Automatically Collected:
- **Browsing Data**: IP address, browser type, operating system
- **General Location**: Country and city (not precise location)
- **Usage Data**: Login times, pages viewed, actions performed
- **Cookies**: For improved user experience (opt-out available)

## 👤 User Rights (GDPR)

### Your Rights as a User:
1. **Right of Access**: Receive a copy of all stored data about you
2. **Right of Rectification**: Correct inaccurate or outdated information
3. **Right of Erasure**: "Right to be forgotten" - delete personal data
4. **Right to Restrict Processing**: Limit certain uses of your data
5. **Right to Data Portability**: Receive data in a transferable format
6. **Right to Object**: Refuse data processing for marketing purposes
7. **Right to Complain**: File a complaint with data protection authorities
